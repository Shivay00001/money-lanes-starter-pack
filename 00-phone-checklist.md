# Money Lanes — Phone Checklist (Day 1)

Sab kuch phone se, zero budget. Har item 10–20 min. Order me karo — fastest money pehle.

## 1. Toloka (same-day microtasks, Payoneer/PayPal payout)
- https://toloka.ai → Sign up → profile complete karo
- "Training tasks" pehle karo, phir qualification quizzes pass karo
- Shuru me $1–5/day normal hai; experienced pe $5–15/day tak jata hai
- Payout: Payoneer connect karo (India me kaam karta hai, no credit card)

## 2. OneForma (global, PayPal monthly)
- https://www.oneforma.com → Join → available projects check karo
- Typical $5–15/hr; kaam project-wise milta hai

## 3. Clickworker (assessments unlock karte hain kaam)
- https://www.clickworker.com → Sign up → assessments complete karo
- Typical $6–12/hr

## 4. TELUS AI Community / Data Partner (India-open AI training)
- https://www.telus.com/en/social-impact/giving-back/telus-digital → AI Community join
- India-specific Data Partner posts ($6–8/hr wale) pe apply karo
- Exam/qualification hoga — yehi asli paisa hai, microtask nahi

## 5. Whop clipping (start today, payout views verify hone ke baad)
- https://whop.com → Google se free signup
- Discover tab → "Content Rewards" search → **The Clip Club** join karo (20,000+ clippers, open campaigns ka hub)
- Funded campaign chuno: rate ($0.20–$6 per 1,000 views), **remaining budget**, rules, allowed platforms check karo
- Detail workflow: `03-whop-clipping-checklist.md`

## 6. DataAnnotation (TRY karo, lekin bharosa mat karo) ⚠️
- https://www.dataannotation.tech → Apply → Starter Assessment (~1 hr)
- **Correction:** multiple 2026 sources kehte hain India hard-blocked hai (US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/IE only). Kuch India job ads dikhe the, isliye try karna worth hai — lekin agar signup pe country reject ho jaye to time waste mat karo, seedha TELUS pe jao.

## 7. Fiverr account banao (GEO gig ke liye)
- https://www.fiverr.com → Join → seller profile setup
- Gig copy ready hai: `01-fiverr-geo-gig-copy.md` — copy-paste karke post karo
- Naya account: pehle 2–3 din me gig live hota hai, pehla order 1–3 week realistic

## Rule
- Koi bhi platform "pay to start" mange → turant chhodo, scam hai.
- Har signup ke baad mujhe batao, mai tracker update karunga.
