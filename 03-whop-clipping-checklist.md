# Whop Clipping — Workflow Checklist

Rates verified 2026: $0.20–$6 per 1,000 verified views (average ~$1). India se ek clipper ne Sept 2026 me 72 hrs me $200+ nikala.

## Setup (ek baar)
1. https://whop.com → Google signup (free)
2. Discover → "Content Rewards" → **The Clip Club** join (open campaigns ka hub)
3. TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts posting account link karo
4. CapCut (free) install karo phone me

## Daily loop (30–45 min)
1. Campaign chuno: **remaining budget > 0** hona chahiye (first-come-first-served)
2. Rules page dhyan se padho: required hashtags, banned claims, allowed platforms
3. Source footage me se 20–60 sec ka viral moment nikalo — **hook pehle 1–2 seconds me**
4. Edit: captions ON (log mute pe dekhte hain), vertical 9:16, ek clip = ek idea
5. Post karo → Whop pe link submit karo → analytics ka screenshot rakho
6. Repeat — volume jeetata hai

## Rejection se bachne ke rules (sabse common reasons)
- Required hashtag/credit missing → reject
- Kisi aur clipper ka near-duplicate → reject (isliye per-post variation rakho)
- Budget khatam hone ke baad submit → reject (pehle budget check karo)
- Galat source material ya banned edit style → reject

## Payout
- Whop views verify karta hai (tracking window) → phir per-1k-views pay
- Withdraw: Whop Payments se
- Finance/tech niches $4–6/1k dete hain; India agency $2/1k pe 1,000+ clippers hire kar rahi hai

## Honest math
- 4 of 6 clips flop hona normal hai (Sept 2026 ke 72-hr test me bhi)
- Ye lottery + volume ka game hai — daily posting ke bina paisa nahi
- YouTube Shorts ad revenue mat dekho (India RPM ₹5–30/1k) — clipping 3–10x better hai per view
