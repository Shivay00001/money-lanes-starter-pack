# AI Labeling — Platform Checklist

Fastest cash lane. Bridge income samjho — asli paisa expert tracks me hai.

## Toloka — https://toloka.ai
- Signup free → profile complete → **training tasks** pehle
- Qualification quizzes pass karo (har quiz ek naya task pool kholta hai)
- Start: $1–5/day → experienced: $5–15/day (India)
- Payout: Payoneer / PayPal / Skrill — Payoneer India me bina credit card ke kaam karta hai

## OneForma — https://www.oneforma.com
- Join free → projects list check karo roz
- $5–15/hr typical, project-wise kaam, PayPal monthly

## Clickworker — https://www.clickworker.com
- Signup → **assessments** complete karo (yehi kaam unlock karte hain)
- $6–12/hr typical

## TELUS AI (India-open, asli paisa) — https://www.telus.com/en/social-impact/giving-back/telus-digital
- AI Community join → **Data Partner** roles pe apply karo
- India posts: $6–8/hr (creative/technical writer); expert postings $20–50/hr tak
- Qualification exam hoga — pass karo to steady kaam

## DataAnnotation — https://www.dataannotation.tech ⚠️
- Coding track $50–100+/hr, PayPal me 3–7 din me payout — **lekin**
- Multiple 2026 sources: India HARD-BLOCKED (sirf US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/IE)
- Kuch Sept-2026 India job ads mile the — isliye ek try banta hai
- Agar signup pe country reject → 2 min me chhodo, TELUS pe focus karo

## Daily routine (jab tak freelance/UGC se bada paisa na aaye)
- Subah: Toloka ke naye tasks (30–45 min)
- Hafte me 2–3 baar: OneForma/Clickworker/TELUS pe naye projects check
- Har payout ka record rakho: platform / date / amount
